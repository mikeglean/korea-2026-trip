NOV 25 (WED) • ICN Airport → Fairfield Seoul Airport Limousine #6007

Airport Limousine Bus #6007 Direct to Fairfield Seoul (6:59 AM / 7:39 AM)

Step 1 (Ticket & Platform): After clearing customs at Incheon T1 Arrivals (1F), claim your Climate Card (1F West Desk across from Exit 13), top up T-Money, change to Heattech, buy Airport Limousine tickets (₩34,000 for 2 pax), and board Limousine Bus #6007 (6:59 AM departure; next bus 7:39 AM).
Step 2 (Under-Bus Luggage): The bus attendant tags and loads all your suitcases directly into the under-bus compartment—zero lifting or station transfers!
Step 3 (Drop-off): Alight at Fairfield by Marriott Seoul (Yeongdeungpo) (~90 mins ride, arrives ~08:30 AM) and store luggage at the front desk before heading to Ssada Gimbap.

Pro Tip: If you take the 7:39 AM #6007 bus, adjust Day 1 timings by +40 mins (cut Myeongdong or COEX Aquarium time, or check in at Fairfield in the afternoon). (Climate Card vs. T-Money Rule: Use your Climate Card for unlimited Seoul subways & Seoul city buses, and use your T-Money Card for Airport Limousine #6007, Busan Metro/buses, and Suwon/Gapyeong rides.)
NOV 28 (SAT) • Fairfield Seoul → Moonbay Busan Line 1 + KTX033 + Taxi

Yeongdeungpo Elevator → Line 1 → Seoul Station KTX033 → Busan Taxi

Step 1 (To KTX): Check out at 10:30 AM, roll bags from Fairfield to Yeongdeungpo Station Exit 5 Elevator, take Line 1 Northbound (11:00–11:25 AM) to Seoul Station, and ride the platform elevator directly to the KTX departure concourse.
Step 2 (On KTX033): 12:16 PM – 2:58 PM (Car 16, Seats 11A & 11B) — place suitcases in the oversized luggage racks at the ends of your coach.
Step 3 (Busan Arrival): Book a taxi from Busan KTX Station (~30 mins, ~₩12,000) straight to Moonbay Hotel (Gwangan) at 3:45 PM to drop bags before Lotte Busan!
