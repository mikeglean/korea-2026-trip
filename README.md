Korea 2026: Mike & Angelu — Trip Companion App

A collaborative, mobile-friendly trip companion web app built for Mike & Angelu's South Korea trip (Nov 25 – Dec 2, 2026) across Seoul, Gapyeong (Nami Island), Busan, and Suwon.

Live app URL: https://mikeglean.github.io/korea-2026-trip/?room=kr2026-x7s6e

Key Features

Documents & Pre-Flight Checklist (Docs): Personal checklists for Mike and Angelu (passports, visa grant notices, PH eTravel, Korea e-Arrival Card, flight/KTX seats, Discover Seoul Pass codes, Climate Card, T-Money, and cards/cash) plus shared hotel vouchers, Klook tour references, and travel kit items.

Day-by-Day Itinerary (Itinerary): Complete schedule for Days 1 to 7 with time blocks, transit/route notes, luggage guidance, map links (Google Maps & Naver Map), completion checkmarks tagged by traveler (Mike or Angelu), inline editing, custom stops, and PDF/photo attachments.

Zero-Lift Luggage & Step-Free Transit Guide (Luggage): Step-by-step transfer notes for Airport Limousine Bus #6007, Line 1 + KTX transfers (KTX033 and KTX-CheongRyong036), hotel bag storage, and Climate Card vs. T-Money usage rules.

Bookings & Hotels (Bookings): Quick summary of confirmed flights (5J 186 and 5J 187), KTX tickets, hotels (Fairfield by Marriott Seoul, Gwangalli Moon Bay Hotel Busan, Hotel GMS Seoul), and Klook Busan tour details.

Food & Free Time (Food): Saved restaurant picks, break-time reminders, and optional neighborhood spots grouped to minimize backtracking.

Discover Seoul Pass (DSP Pass): Tracker for 72-hour Mobile Discover Seoul Pass attractions and perks starting from the first scan at Changdeokgung Palace.

Budget, Currency Converter & Expense Log (Budget): Pre-paid booking summary, recommended on-trip KRW/PHP costing, a live KRW (₩) ↔ PHP (₱) converter, and a synced on-trip expense tracker supporting Cash, T-Money, and Card (BDO VISA, HSBC, UNIONBANK).

Using & Syncing Between Mike and Angelu

Select Traveler Profile: Tap 👨🏻 Mike or 👩🏻 Angelu under Checking as: so checkmarks and logged expenses record who updated them.

Sync Updates:

Cloud Sync / Sync Button: Tap Sync in the top header to pull/push updates when connected to your configured cloud bin/endpoint.

Quick Sync Code (Fallback / Offline): Open Share, tap Copy My Sync Code to share via chat, or paste a code and tap Paste & Apply Code on the other phone.

Add to Home Screen:

iPhone (Safari): Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): Tap ⋮ → Add to Home Screen (or Install app).
